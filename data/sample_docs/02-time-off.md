# Time Off Policy

Rest is part of doing good work. This policy explains the leave available to full-time employees.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at two
days per month. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount.

You can carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days expire if they
are not used by the end of March.

## Requesting leave

Request annual leave in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences of three days or
more. Shorter absences only need one business day of notice. Your manager approves requests, and
the team calendar updates automatically once a request is approved.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry over. For absences
longer than three consecutive days, a doctor's note is required.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive
6 weeks. Parental leave can be taken within the first year after a birth or adoption and may be
split into up to two blocks.

## Public holidays

Lumen Labs observes the public holidays of the country you are employed in. If you work on a public
holiday because of an on-call incident, you receive a replacement day off.
