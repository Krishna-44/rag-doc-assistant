# Travel and Expenses

This policy applies to all business travel and work-related purchases.

## Booking travel

Book flights and hotels through the company travel tool so that the travel team can reach you in an
emergency. Economy class is the default. Flights longer than eight hours may be booked in premium
economy.

## Meals and per diem

When travelling, you can spend up to 75 USD per day on meals for domestic trips and up to 100 USD
per day for international trips. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client dinners approved in
advance by a director.

## Receipts and submitting claims

Keep an itemised receipt for any expense over 25 USD. Submit expense claims in the finance portal
within 30 days of the purchase. Claims submitted later than 30 days may be rejected.

## Approvals

Your manager approves expenses up to 1,000 USD. Any single expense above 1,000 USD also needs
approval from a director before you make the purchase.

## Company cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate card. Personal purchases
on a corporate card are not allowed, even if you intend to pay them back.
