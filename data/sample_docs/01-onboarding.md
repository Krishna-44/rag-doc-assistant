# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Lumen Labs. This guide covers what happens in your first weeks. Lumen Labs is a
fictional company used as sample data for this project.

## Your first day

On your first day you will receive a laptop that has already been enrolled in device management.
Sign in with the temporary password from your welcome email and change it immediately using the
company password manager. IT runs a live setup session at 10:00 every Monday for new joiners.

You will be added to the main chat workspace, the engineering wiki and your team's issue tracker.
If an account is missing after your first day, open a ticket in the IT help desk queue.

## Onboarding buddy

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team. Your buddy is not your
manager: they are the person to ask the questions you feel awkward asking anyone else. Plan to meet
your buddy at least twice a week during your first month.

## The 30-60-90 day plan

Your manager will share a 30-60-90 day plan in your first week.

- **First 30 days:** learn the product, set up your development environment and ship one small
  change to production.
- **Days 31 to 60:** take ownership of a medium-sized task end to end, including the design review.
- **Days 61 to 90:** join the on-call rotation as a shadow and lead one feature.

## Probation and check-ins

The probation period is three months. Your manager holds a formal check-in at the end of month one
and month three, and you can raise concerns at any time through your HR partner.
