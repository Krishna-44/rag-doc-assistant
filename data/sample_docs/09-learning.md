# Learning and Development

Lumen Labs invests in helping people grow their skills and careers.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a personal learning budget of 1,500 USD per year. You can spend it on courses,
books, certifications or conference tickets that are relevant to your role. Unused budget does not
roll over to the next year.

## Learning time

Engineers can spend up to 10% of their working time on learning, usually on Friday afternoons. Use
it to explore a new technology, contribute to open source or prepare an internal tech talk.

## Conferences

If a talk you submitted to a conference is accepted, the company pays for your travel and ticket
from the events budget, not from your personal learning budget. Share what you learned in an
internal write-up after the event.

## Mentorship

The mentorship program pairs employees with a mentor from another department for six months.
Sign-ups open twice a year, in January and July.

## Internal tech talks

Tech talks happen every other Wednesday at 15:00 and are recorded for people in other time zones.
Interns are encouraged to present their projects at the end of their internship.
