# Remote and Hybrid Work

Lumen Labs is a hybrid company. Most teams work from home on some days and meet in the office on
others.

## Office days and core hours

Teams that are based near an office meet in person on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Everyone is expected
to be available during core hours, 11:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone. Outside core hours you
can arrange your day as you like, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

## Home office equipment

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD for a desk, chair, monitor or
other equipment. The company also reimburses up to 50 USD per month for home internet.
Equipment bought with the stipend belongs to you.

## Working from another country

You may work from a country other than the one you are employed in for up to 30 days per calendar
year. You need written approval from your manager and the People team before you travel, because
working abroad can create tax and immigration obligations.

## Communication norms

Prefer written, asynchronous communication for decisions so that people in other time zones can
follow along. Keep your chat status up to date, and write meeting notes in the shared wiki so that
anyone who missed the meeting can catch up.
